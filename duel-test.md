# Duel tournament — how the winning line was picked

Two rival drafters each wrote 10 candidate pickup lines, so the tournament
started with 20 entries. Every candidate was judged with the kit's own brief
as the rubric, in head-to-head duels run in both directions (each pair
compared twice, order swapped) to cancel out any first-option bias.

The garbage-collection line — candidate A1 — won the bracket with a duel
mean of 0.69, clearing the 0.60 bar for submission. The two closest
challengers were A5, the backup-and-restore line (199 characters), and A8,
the log-file line (188 characters); both were strong but lost their duels
against A1.

A rival critic then attacked the winner, and three fixes went in:

- The phrase "every ex" was cut: it read as creepy rather than charming,
  and the line is funnier without it.
- "deleted" became "freed", which is what garbage collection actually
  does — the machine vocabulary stays accurate.
- "which means" was replaced by a colon, tightening the punchline.

The final line is 182 characters long and is the text shipped in main.ts.
